# AmazingTalker

> AmazingTalker is an online tutoring marketplace (founded 2016, Taipei) connecting students with vetted professional tutors for personalized 1-on-1 lessons in 60+ languages plus academic, music, and lifestyle subjects. It publicly exposes a ChatGPT-plugin OpenAPI for finding tutors.

## APIs
- [Find Tutors plugin OpenAPI](https://en.amazingtalker.com/.well-known/openapi.yaml): OpenAPI 3.0.1, one public no-auth operation (findTeachers) that returns tutors for a subject with price/tag/language/location filters.
